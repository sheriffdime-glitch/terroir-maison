La Maison

Three rules, never bent
I

One farm per line

If a grower cannot fill the shelf, the shelf stays empty. Blending sources is how a label stops meaning anything.

II

Picked to order

We buy against the box count, not a forecast. What remains at closing goes to the kitchen, never to the bin.

III

Twelve hours cold

Field to hall in half a day, held at four degrees. The date on the label is the day it was picked.

Correspondence

The harvest list, written on Thursday evenings and sent before the doors open. One letter a week, nothing else.
